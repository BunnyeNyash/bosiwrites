👋 **Hi again!** It's me. Bosi.

Today I wanted to give you all a breakdown of the *core skills* that I’m going to be building throughout the course of my learning journey to thrive in the field. The world of Fintech is dynamic and interdisciplinary, and building the right skills is key. As it blends finance with technology, the learning path is going to cover a bit of both. Here’s what I will be focusing on:

**Finance Skills**
Fintech starts with a strong understanding of the financial world, and I will be focusing on the following:
  - **Financial Systems:** How banking, payments, lending, and investment markets work.
  - **Accounting Principles:** Basics of bookkeeping, financial statements, and ratios.
  - **Risk Management:** Understanding financial risks and tools to manage them.
  - **Corporate Finance:** Valuation, funding, and capital allocation strategies.
  - **Wealth Management:** Personalized investment and savings strategies.

**💻 Technical Skills**
Tech knowledge is the backbone of fintech, blending creativity and problem-solving. Here are some of the technical skills that I plan on building:
  - **Programming:** Python, Java, SQL, JavaScript, and R for finance-specific applications.
  - **Data Science & Analytics:** Advanced Excel, Tableau, Power BI, and machine learning models.
  - **APIs:** Integrating systems using RESTful APIs for seamless fintech solutions.
  - **Software Development:** Building secure, scalable financial apps.
  - **Cloud Computing:** Platforms like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud for fintech deployment.
  - **Cybersecurity:** Encryption, fraud detection, and secure systems to protect financial data.

**🌐 Web Development Skills**
Web development plays a vital role in delivering fintech solutions to users. Here are some of the skills that I'll be diving into:
  - **Frontend Development:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and frameworks like React.js or Angular for designing responsive and interactive interfaces.
  - **Backend Development:** Server-side programming with Python (Django, Flask), Node.js, or Ruby on Rails.
  - **Database Management:** MySQL, PostgreSQL, or MongoDB for handling financial data.
  - **UI/UX Design:** Designing intuitive, user-friendly financial platforms with tools like Figma or Adobe XD.
  - **Payment Gateway Integration:** Adding secure and seamless payment systems like Stripe, M-Pesa, or PayPal.

**📊 Data Skills**
Data powers fintech innovations. The key areas that I will focus on include:
  - **Big Data:** Working with Hadoop, Spark, or Snowflake for handling large datasets.
  - **Data Visualization:** Presenting insights clearly with tools like Looker, Power BI and Tableau.
  - **Predictive Analytics:** Building models to forecast trends, risks, and behaviors.

**Fintech-Specific Knowledge**
Mastering the unique aspects of fintech is also crucial, and I will be exploring the following topics:
  - **Blockchain & Cryptocurrency:** Understanding distributed ledgers and digital currencies.
  - **Digital Payments:** Mobile wallets, contactless payments, and peer-to-peer systems.
  - **Robo-Advisors:** Algorithms for automated investment management.
  - **InsurTech:** Technology innovations in insurance.
  - **RegTech:** Tools that simplify compliance with financial regulations.
  - **Lending Tech:** Online lending platforms and credit scoring models.

**🔐 Legal & Compliance Knowledge**
Knowing the rules is critical in this regulated field. For this one, I'll venture into:
  - **KYC/AML:** Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering are some of the compliance processes that I'll be exploring. They are used for preventing fraud and money laundering.
  - **Financial Regulations:** PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2), GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), and other global regulatory frameworks.
  - **Digital Identity Management:** Secure authentication systems for fintech users.

**Soft Skills**
The human side of fintech is just as important, and I'll consider looking into:
  - **Problem-Solving:** Creative solutions for customer pain points.
  - **Collaboration:** Working with cross-functional teams, including finance and tech experts.
  - **Communication:** Explaining complex topics in simple terms for clients or stakeholders.
  - **Adaptability:** Staying up to date in a rapidly changing field.

I’m excited to learn, practice, and document these skills step by step, so stay tuned for more. Got any insights or suggestions? Let’s connect and exchange ideas!
