**Fintech 101** <br>
Fintech (short for Financial Technology) is all about using technology to improve financial services. It's basically all the software, mobile applications, and other technologies that power the usage of financial services from savings, to investments, to lending ... Think of it as giving your old-school bank a tech upgrade that's faster, smarter, and more accessible.

In simpler terms:

Fintech = Finance + Technology = Your money, but with swag.

**Why We Should Care about Fintech, especially as Africans** <br>
Here’s the deal: In many parts of Africa, traditional banking hasn’t reached everyone. But guess what? Almost everyone has a phone! That’s where fintech steps in, making financial services more inclusive and accessible.

Here are a few ways fintech is changing the game:

Mobile Money: Remember how life was before M-Pesa, Airtel Money, or MTN MoMo? Hard, right? Mobile money lets you send and receive cash without needing a bank account. Perfect for that rural area where the nearest bank is a day’s walk away.
Online Lending: Need a quick loan? Apps like Tala and Branch provide small loans without the headache of long paperwork or waiting weeks for approval. Just a few taps, and boom, money in your account.
Savings and Investments: Platforms like PiggyVest (Nigeria) and Chumz (Kenya) make saving and investing as easy as sending a text. Want to start saving for that new boda boda? They’ve got your back.
Payments and Transfers: From buying groceries via PesaLink to paying for your Netflix subscription, fintech simplifies payments. No more long queues or fumbling with cash.

Tools of the Trade: What Powers Fintech?
Fintech relies on cool tech to make magic happen. Here are the superheroes behind the scenes:
  - **Mobile Apps📱:** Where the magic happens for you as a user.
  - **Blockchain⛓️:** The tech behind cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin.
  - **AI and Data🤖📊:** To predict your spending habits and offer personalized services.
  - **APIs🔌:** Think of these as bridges that allow apps and banks to talk to each other.

Fintech isn’t just about money; it’s about empowerment. Whether you’re a business owner looking to expand, a farmer saving for better seeds, or a student paying your school fees via an app, fintech is here to make life easier.
