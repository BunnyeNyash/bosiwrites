Welcome to **BosiWrites**. Your privacy matters to me, so this page explains what information may be collected when you visit the blog, how it may be used, and the third-party services that may be involved.

## Information We Collect

You can visit and read BosiWrites without providing any personal information.

If you choose to subscribe to the blog, you will provide your email address so that you can receive updates from BosiWrites.

If you choose to contact me through the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, and message. The form is handled by Formspree, which receives and processes the submission and sends a notification to the email address I use for BosiWrites.

BosiWrites is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect and log visitors' IP addresses when they access a GitHub Pages site for security purposes. I do not use this information to personally identify or track visitors.

Apart from information you choose to provide through the site's forms or subscription features, BosiWrites does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors.

## Cookies and Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Third-Party Links

BosiWrites may use third-party services to support different parts of the website, such as analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other site functionality.

Some pages may also contain links to websites or services that are not operated by me. Once you leave BosiWrites and visit a third-party website, that website's own privacy policy and terms will apply.

I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

## How Your Information Is Used

Information collected through the site may be used to:
  - keep the website functioning properly;
  - understand how visitors interact with the blog;
  - improve the site's content and user experience;
  - respond to messages or enquiries; and
  - support advertising and related website services where applicable.

I do not sell your personal information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time as BosiWrites grows, new features are added, or the services used by the site change.

Any updates will be posted on this page, along with a new "Last updated" date.
