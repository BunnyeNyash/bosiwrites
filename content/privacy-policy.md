Welcome to **BosiWrites**. Your privacy matters to me, so this page explains what information may be collected when you visit the blog, how it may be used, and the third-party services that may be involved.
<br>
## Information We Collect

You can visit and read BosiWrites without providing any personal information.

If you choose to subscribe to the blog, you will provide your email address so that you can receive updates from BosiWrites.

If you choose to contact me through the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, and message. The form is handled by Formspree, which receives and processes the submission and sends a notification to the email address I use for BosiWrites.

BosiWrites is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect and log visitors' IP addresses when they access a GitHub Pages site for security purposes. I do not use this information to personally identify or track visitors.

Apart from information you choose to provide through the site's forms or subscription features, BosiWrites does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors.
<br>
## Cookies and Advertising
BosiWrites does not use cookies for tracking visitors or personalized advertising.

I use GoatCounter to understand which pages and posts are getting attention and to see how visitors are interacting with the blog. GoatCounter is a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies or store persistent tracking identifiers. It may collect information such as the page visited, browser, operating system, approximate location, language, screen size, and referring website in order to provide aggregate statistics.

GoatCounter does not store visitors' IP addresses or full browser User-Agent information in its database. For session counting, this information may be held temporarily in memory, but it is not stored on disk.

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
If any new services or features are added to BosiWrites that collect information or use cookies or similar technologies, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
